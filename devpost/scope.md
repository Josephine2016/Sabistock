# SabiStock

A simple stock-restocking tool for small phone and computer accessory shops.

## The Unique Kernel
An owner records stock coming in and taps Sold -1 when an item sells. SabiStock puts the items with fewer than five left in a red Low Stock list, with the lowest quantities first, so the owner can see what to restock without guessing or checking every shelf.

## Who It's For
Small business owners with no more than 10 people, starting with phone-accessories outlets, mobile phone shops, and computer-accessories shops. They need to know what to restock and want a quick alternative to checking shelves and relying on memory.

## The Core Loop
The owner opens the app, adds items with a photo and quantity, and taps Sold -1 as items sell. The dashboard updates quantities and puts low-stock items first, giving the owner a short restock list to check again as stock changes.

## Inspiration & Identity
Not established.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner wants to help small business owners run more efficiently and know what to restock instead of guessing.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a demo, an owner opens the app and immediately sees a red Low Stock list with the lowest quantities first—for example, an iPhone Charger at 2, Phone Pouch at 1, and Screen Guard at 3. They tap Sold -1 on two items and see the quantities update live, then add a new item with a photo. In less than a minute, they know what to restock, rather than spending about 15 minutes checking shelves. The sorted low-stock list and live quantity change are the visible proof.

## The POC Boundary
For one small phone or computer-accessories shop: add an item with a photo, name, and quantity; record a sale with Sold -1; and show a live-updating red Low Stock list for quantities below five, with the lowest quantities first. The demo should show the owner what to restock in less than a minute.
